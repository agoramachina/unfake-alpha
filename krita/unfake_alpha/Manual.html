Unfake Alpha

For generated images that come with a painted transparency checkerboard (or a flat painted background): it rebuilds the checker behind every pixel, treats grey pixels between the checker's two shades as background (which also takes the AI watermark's pixel nudges with it), and runs "colour to alpha" on everything else, so glows keep their soft edges as real transparency.

Tools > Scripts > Unfake Alpha... with the image's paint layer selected (8-bit RGBA). The result goes on a new layer above it; the original is only hidden. To undo, delete the new layer.

  • Background: find the checkerboard (automatic), or a flat colour such as #000000.
  • Floor: opacity under this becomes fully transparent (default 0.03, 3%).

Limits: grey art on a grey checker can't be told apart from it; soft hazes come out a little fainter than painted (the safe guess for opacity is the least that explains each pixel). The full walkthrough is in the README.
